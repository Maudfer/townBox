# Work Actions — Content Planning Catalog

Planning input for the upcoming Actions system (things people do while at work): a shared catalog of **continuous** actions (status-like activities that span time) and **discrete** actions (granular, log-worthy one-shot events), mapped to every job title from [`businesses-and-jobs.md`](./businesses-and-jobs.md).

**Verified counts (script-generated):**

- Distinct continuous work actions: **505**
- Distinct discrete work actions: **508**
- Total distinct action names: **1013**
- Job titles covered: **213** (each with >=5 continuous and >=5 discrete role actions, plus the shared General workday set)

**Naming conventions:**

- Continuous actions are gerund phrases — "Stocking shelves", "Attending patients", "Auditing accounts". They describe an ongoing status.
- Discrete actions are past-tense log lines — "Dropped a tray", "Greeted a customer", "Misplaced a document". They read naturally in an event log or the city feed.

**Sharing model.** Actions are organized into named **theme groups**. A job draws its action pool from one or more themes, so actions are shared across jobs wherever sensible (every register job shares "Ringing up purchases"; every kitchen job shares "Burned a batch"). One special theme, **General workday**, applies to *every* job on top of its role themes — it holds the universal mundane filler ("Complained about the time", "Refilled the coffee mug").
