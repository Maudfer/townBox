Can you help me improve the simulation dynamics for this city builder/sim? First, some context. Thise is not your average sim city clone. It's heavily focused on person simulation, down to individual actions and lore generation, heavily inspired by Dwarf Fortress.

Before we begin, please read the entirety of README.md and CLAUDE.md so you get a good understanding of the simulation systems. I've spent a large amount of time by now adding frameworks and systems to provide an imense granularity of detail to this simulation, but not focusing on the "glue" that makes it all work together. I've added Objects to our simulated world, which people can carry, take, give, through Actions, which are also used for executing their Jobs, which require Skills and are run by Businesses, and people's lives have Events, and so on. Actions can range from simple momentaneous things like picking up something to continuous activities like working, through different types of actions that can contain other actions, its very powerful. But it's kinda all over the place in practice, I'm gonna explain why in a list down this text.

We have a pre-generated data asset system where I have some people's lives pre-simulated non-visually. I strongly recommend you create a small script to uncompress the files for like 3 or 4 people in src/history/, so you can look at their actions history and events history, so you get an understanding of what I'm talking about. 

I'm gonna list my gripes here, and I'd like you to try and think of overarching solutions, from small adjustments to reworks of existing systems to entirely new supporting systems, to passes of data improvement to our existing Actions and Events, and even adding a bunch of new Actions and Events (possibly gonna need a number of all of these solutions to tackle all of these problems), and write it in a proposal in docs/. Try to spend some time really thinking how to solve these creatively, let's make the game's sim people look more alive! My list:

- People do a lot of things that aren't really connected, very random things. Having a bit of that in a sim like this is fine because it makes for quirky and funny logs on them, but too much looks just random (which to be fair it kinda is, a lot of the time, in the current implementation).
- People do things to other people that other people don't necessarily react to, and that sometimes have no consequence. If someone gives someone an Apple, the other person should receive an Apple, both as an event log and as a practical consequence (the apple should move from one to another). This doesnt apply only to object giving. We need to evaluate every action we have, do a full audit, evaluating which ones involve or affect other people somehow, and evaluate if they need revision. I have subitems for this one:
            - Need to see if actions that semantically involve other people are being performed only in the presence of other people. Actions that semantically involve objects like skating should require a skateboard on the inventory.
            - If they are performed towards specific person, is the person chosen prior to this action, smartly? For example, you'd only kiss someone you are in a relationship with, not just anyone. Also are people in the same building when you are doing it to them? Is there a logic to plan and go to where that person is to do important stuff (like proposing marriage)? 
            - I also feel like actions are lacking contextual importance evaluation. Some actions you just do because you are there. Some actions you do because they are part of other continuous actions. Some you want or need to do, some with a set cadence, some because of context. Maybe that's what we are missing, a way to represent wants and needs? Or people are not hungry, they do not have needs. And if they did, right now, eating would not necessarily be connected to cooking, which would not be connected to buying food objects. We don't even have a market.
            - People mostly do things inside buildings right now. This makes the simulation visually dull. There are a lot of things people should be doing on the street. They should be able to go jogging, clean their sidewalk, hangout with their companions as a couple walking together (another example of action that should be coordinated between both and use the consent system, that may need an upgrade by the way), just go for a walk alone, do things like ride skateboard (no need for visuals right now, just show them on the street moving, but the continuous action should be showing what they are doing).
            - Also a lot of Events have no consequence. For example, getting sick doesn't prevent a person for working or going out, and it might get cured an hour later. It's weird, everything feels loose and unconnected.
- People often hold much more Objects than it makes sense to. And they have no intentionality grabbing objects most of the time.
- We are not showing what people are currently doing anywhere, just a log in their window.
- If you start a game and place a couple houses and a couple businesses, it takes a long time for someone to get a Job. Needs balancing, maybe needs to keep track of the next people we are adding to houses from our asset pool of generated people, and morph existing and new business buildings to businesses that make sense for them? Or may an entrepenurial system where people start businesses? Note, at this point the goal is to make the simulation flow neatly. Don't worry about challenge to the player, we don't even have managerial aspects yet. I want the player to start putting things down and for things to start happening.
- We have no concept of public services, and people don't miss them. Police, hospital, etc. And I think the offline asset pool gets in the way of that also, because people that come from the asset pool can not meet the criteria for the needs of the town, like being a police officer. I created the asset pool so people moving into the town would not be blank slates without family or histories. Maybe we need to relax that a little, use the history and family aspects, but make people more maleable to context, even injecting history in them when bringing them into the simulation, like career transitions and skills acquisitions to match.
- We have no tracking of city needs, like lacking police, lacking firemen, lacking garbage collection, lacking doctors, lacking jail.
- We have no concept of bad stuff, people don't get depressed, don't steal, don't use alcohol. And by the way these things should be coherent. A death in the family should have more probability to induce alcohol consumption, which also shouldn't be bad per se, but in excess. And stealing should be a result of financial struggle. And police should chase (even visually, on the street), and criminals should run. Etc.

I realize this is a big one. I expect a large group of proposals, very detailed. Take your time. I want to solve these. The one restriction, I don't want proposals that involve reducing or streamlining the number of actions, events, objects, etc. I know we have a ton, but we need to audit and zgradually improve on the data we have, we need to give the engine more expressive power, and we need to create glue to make them work together. No reducing. If anything, a lot of what I asked here involves adding a lot more of all of these, and auditing and reworking the hundreds or thousands that exist. Big effort, I know. But I want to make this a very ambitious simulation, detailed like none before. The Dwarf Fortress of city sims. Please give it your best! Think about it architechturally, but also down to detail level, addressing all of my examples and gripes there. And produce a big doc.
